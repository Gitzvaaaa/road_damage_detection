2.6.2. Data Augmentation
Data augmentation generates varied training inputs through transformations intended to preserve 
class identity. Random flips, modest geometric changes, and photometric changes expose the 
model to variations that may occur in unseen images. Transformations must remain appropriate to 
the task; for example, large rotations can change the directional meaning of a road crack [1, Sec. 
7.4].
The project applies online horizontal flipping, small affine transformations, color jitter, occasional 
perspective distortion, and Gaussian blur before tensor conversion and normalization. Augmentation 
is restricted to training images. Validation and test images receive only tensor conversion and 
normalization, using statistics computed from the training set. This preserves a consistent evaluation 
procedure and avoids using validation or test statistics to prepare the training inputs [12].
