Macro averaging gives equal weight to every class, while weighted averaging gives more influence 
to frequent classes. Macro-F1 is the mean of the class F1 scores, not generally the harmonic mean 
of macro-precision and macro-recall. Reporting per-class and macro scores helps expose weak 
minority-class performance that accuracy or weighted scores can conceal [7].
