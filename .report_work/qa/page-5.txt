2.3.4. Overfitting and Regularization
Overfitting occurs when a model fits the training data more closely than is useful for predicting 
unseen examples. A widening training–validation performance gap can indicate overfitting. 
Regularization reduces this risk through constraints on learning or additional variability during 
training [1, Ch. 7].
